# Rice brown spot

## Signs on the leaf

Tiny spots that become oval or cylindrical, dark brown with a yellow halo, mostly on the leaf blade; spots merge and the leaf dries. Nurseries look scorched; dark spots on grain husks.

## What favours it

25-30 C with humidity above 80%; excess nitrogen makes it worse. Carried on infected seed and stubble and on weed hosts.

## What to do

- Field sanitation: remove weed hosts and infected debris.
- Use slow-release nitrogen fertilizers.
- Grow resistant/moderately resistant varieties (page lists CO 51, CO 52, CO 53, CO 55, CO 56, ASD 18, IR 64).
- Use disease-free seed.

## Sprays named by the source

The source names these treatments, quoted exactly. Use them only after your KVK confirms the diagnosis and the dose for your crop stage:

- "Treat the seeds with Thiram or Captan at 4 g/kg."
- "Spray the nursery with Edifenphos 40 ml or Mancozeb 80 g for 20 cent nursery."
- "Spray the crop in the main field with Edifenphos 200 ml or Mancozeb 500 g when grade reaches 3. If needed repeat after 15 days."

## When to get help

Before buying or spraying anything, show the plant or this photo to your Krishi Vigyan Kendra, or call the Kisan Call Centre free on 1800-180-1551. Doses vary by state, variety and crop stage.
