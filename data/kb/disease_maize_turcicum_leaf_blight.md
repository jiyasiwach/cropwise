# Maize turcicum (northern corn) leaf blight

## Signs on the leaf

Long cigar-shaped grey-green to tan lesions, first on lower leaves, running parallel to the leaf edge (about 1-6 inches); they merge, and leaves turn grey-green and brittle as if frost-killed; dark fuzzy spore growth under lesions.

## What favours it

Wet, humid, cool weather, usually later in the season. Survives on infected crop debris; spores spread by wind.

## What to do

- Burn or bury infected maize stubble.

## Sprays named by the source

The source names these treatments, quoted exactly. Use them only after your KVK confirms the diagnosis and the dose for your crop stage:

- "Spray mancozeb or zineb @ 2-4 g/l or propiconazole 25% EC @ 1ml/l on 35 and 50 DAS"
