# Grape downy mildew

## Signs on the leaf

Pale yellow-green patches on leaves that turn brown; badly hit leaves fall early. Shoots, tendrils and petioles may curl and die; young berries get white fungal growth. (TNAU adds: yellowish see-through spots on the upper leaf with white downy growth beneath.)

## What favours it

Wet winter followed by wet spring and warm summer with on-off rain; survives in fallen leaves and spreads by rain splash. (TNAU: optimum 20-22 C, RH 80-100%.)

## What to do

- Prune vines after the second week of October; remove and destroy affected parts at pruning.
- Collect downy-mildew-infected leaves and put them in a compost pit.
- Tolerant/resistant varieties listed: Amber Queen, Dogridge, Red Sultana (downy mildew); Bangalore blue, Concord, Champion, Catawba (powdery + downy).
