# Mango powdery mildew

## Signs on the leaf

White, surface powdery fungal growth on leaves, flower stalks, flowers and young fruit; young leaves hit on both sides, patches merge and turn purplish-brown; flowers and fruit drop early.

## What favours it

Rain or mist with cooler nights during flowering. Survives in dormant buds.

## What to do

- Avoid spraying at full bloom.
- Prune diseased leaves and malformed panicles to reduce inoculum.
