# Rice bacterial leaf blight

## Signs on the leaf

Water-soaked, see-through streaks start at the leaf edge, spread with a wavy margin and turn straw-yellow, then white, over the whole leaf. Milky bacterial droplets on young lesions in the morning dry to a white crust. Seedlings can wilt completely (kresek).

## What favours it

Clipping seedling tips at transplanting, heavy rain or dew, flooding/deep water, strong wind, 25-30 C, and too much nitrogen (especially late top-dressing). Spreads through irrigation water and rainstorms.

## What to do

- Burn the stubbles.
- Use optimum (not excess) fertilizer.
- Do not clip seedling tips at transplanting.
- Avoid flooded conditions.
- Remove weed hosts periodically.

## Sprays named by the source

The source names these treatments, quoted exactly. Use them only after your KVK confirms the diagnosis and the dose for your crop stage:

- "Seed treatment: Soaking of seeds in streptomycin sulphate 90% and tetracycline 10% SP @ 40 ppm for 12 hours or seedling root dipping @ 40 ppm for 30 minutes ."
