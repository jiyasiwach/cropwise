# Farmer helplines

## Kisan Call Centre — 1800-180-1551

Any farming question, answered by agriculture graduates; escalated to subject experts.

Hours: 06:00-22:00 IST, all days. Cost: free. More: https://mkisan.gov.in/

## PM-KISAN helpline — 155261

PM-KISAN instalment not received, eKYC, beneficiary status.

Hours: office hours. Cost: free. More: https://pmkisan.gov.in/

## PMFBY crop insurance helpline — 14447

Crop insurance enrolment, claim status, loss intimation (within 72 hours of damage).

Hours: office hours. Cost: free. More: https://pmfby.gov.in/

## Soil Health Card helpline — 011-24305591

Soil testing, getting or reprinting a Soil Health Card.

Hours: office hours. Cost: standard call rate. More: https://soilhealth.dac.gov.in/

If CropWise cannot answer a question, or the answer involves a pesticide dose, a payment problem or a legal matter, call the Kisan Call Centre on 1800-180-1551. It is free and answers in 22 languages.
