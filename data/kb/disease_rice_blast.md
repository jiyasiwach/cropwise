# Rice blast

## Signs on the leaf

Small bluish-green flecks on leaves that enlarge into spindle-shaped spots with grey centres and dark brown borders; spots merge and leaves dry out; badly hit nurseries look burnt. Also black lesions on nodes and grey-brown neck lesions that make panicles break or fall.

## What favours it

Intermittent drizzle, cloudy weather, very high humidity (93-99%), cool nights (15-20 C, or below 26 C), weed collateral hosts nearby, and excess nitrogen.

## What to do

- Grow resistant/moderately resistant varieties (page lists CO47, CO 51, CO 52, CO 53, CO55, CO56, ASD 18, IR64).
- Remove and destroy weed hosts on bunds and channels.
- Avoid excess nitrogen (listed as a favouring factor).

## Sprays named by the source

The source names these treatments, quoted exactly. Use them only after your KVK confirms the diagnosis and the dose for your crop stage:

- "Treat the seeds with Captan or Thiram or Carbendazim or Tricyclazole at 2 g/kg or Bacillus subtilis @ 10g/kg of seed."
- "Spray the nursery with Carbendazim 50 WP @ 25 g or Edifenphos 50 EC @ 25 ml for 20 cent nursery."
- "Foliar spray the main field with edifenphos 200 ml or carbendazim 100 g or tricyclazole 75 WP @ 200 g or iprobenphos (IBP) 200 ml /acre or azoxystrobin + difenoconazole 100 ml/acre when initial symptoms are noticed."
