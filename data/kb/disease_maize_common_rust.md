# Maize common rust

## Signs on the leaf

Tiny flecks on both leaf surfaces that become round to long, cinnamon-brown, powdery raised pustules on both sides; pustules turn brownish-black as the crop matures.

## What favours it

Cool-to-warm moist weather (15-25 C). Spreads from Oxalis alternate hosts and by wind-borne spores.

## What to do

- Remove alternate hosts (Oxalis species).
- Collect crop remains and destroy by burning or burying.

## Sprays named by the source

The source names these treatments, quoted exactly. Use them only after your KVK confirms the diagnosis and the dose for your crop stage:

- "Foliar spray of kresoxim-methyl 44.3% SC @ 1 ml/l or tebuconazole @ 1ml/l orchlorothalonil or mancozeb 2 ml/l at 35 and 50 DAS"

## When to get help

Before buying or spraying anything, show the plant or this photo to your Krishi Vigyan Kendra, or call the Kisan Call Centre free on 1800-180-1551. Doses vary by state, variety and crop stage.
