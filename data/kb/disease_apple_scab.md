# Apple scab

## Signs on the leaf

Olive spots on the leaf underside that turn dark brown to black and velvety; on young leaves the spots have feathery, radiating edges; badly infected leaves curl and distort. Fruit get small, rough, black, corky spots.

## What favours it

Rainy, cool spring weather; spores come from fallen infected leaves and are spread by rain splash and wind.

## What to do

- Clean cultivation: collect and destroy fallen leaves and pruned material in winter.

## Sprays named by the source

The source names these treatments, quoted exactly. Use them only after your KVK confirms the diagnosis and the dose for your crop stage:

- "Spray Tridemorph 0.1% before flowering."
- "Spray Mancozeb 0.25 % at bearing stage."
- "Spray 5 % urea prior to leaf fall in autumn and 2 % before bud break to hasten the decomposition of leaves."
