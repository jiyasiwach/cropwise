# Citrus greening (huanglongbing)

## Signs on the leaf

Small, sparse leaves and twig die-back; blotchy yellowing (mottling) that looks like zinc deficiency; leaves become leathery with prominent veins, dull olive green, sometimes with green circular dots. Fruit small, lopsided, stays partly green, poor juice.

## What favours it

No weather conditions given on the page. Spread by infected budwood and by the psyllid Diaphorina citri.

## What to do

- Use pathogen-free budwood for propagation.

## Sprays named by the source

The source names these treatments, quoted exactly. Use them only after your KVK confirms the diagnosis and the dose for your crop stage:

- "Control psyllids with insecticides."
- "500 ppm tetracycline spray, requires fortnightly application."
