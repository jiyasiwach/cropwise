How it works

Four steps, about a minute, and nothing to type that you do not already know.

1

Say where you are

A town name is enough. We find the coordinates ourselves.

2

We read the ground

Nitrogen, pH, clay and organic carbon from ISRIC SoilGrids, and we say how far away the reading was taken.

3

We read the sky

Mean temperature, humidity and the rain that has actually fallen, from Open-Meteo.

4

You get a plan

Three crops with reasons, then dates, fertiliser, shops and the best mandi to sell at.

Built for the whole season

Not just "grow rice" — the decisions that follow it.

🌾 Three crops, with reasons

Each one says why: rainfall, pH, humidity. 97% accurate from a town name, 99.6% with a soil test.

📅 Sowing and harvest dates

Worked out from today, not from a season name. "Sow by 30 November, harvest 2 February."

🧪 Fertiliser in bags

Not kilograms of nutrient — how many 50 kg bags of urea, DAP and MOP to buy for your acres.

🏪 Shops near you

Agri-input dealers from OpenStreetMap, nearest first, with directions and a phone number.

💰 The mandi that pays most

Ranked by what you keep after transport. The highest price is often not the best one.

📈 Profit per acre

Expected yield at today's price, minus seed, fertiliser and labour, compared across your three crops.

💬 KrishiMitra

Ask by voice in six languages. Every answer cites its source, and says so when it does not know.

📊 Measured, not claimed

Retrieval scores, guardrail results and model accuracy are published in the app, on the /eval page.
