The problem CropWise works on

Four decisions a small farmer makes every season, usually without data. Every figure below links to its public source; where we could not find one we could check, we say so.

Who it is for

About 86% of India's farm holdings are small or marginal (under 2 hectares), and the average holding is 1.08 hectares, down from 1.15 in 2010-11. On a farm that size one wrong crop, one extra bag of urea or one bad sale is a large share of the year's income.

Sources: Agriculture Census 2015-16, as cited by the Ministry of Agriculture in PIB, Lok Sabha reply and PIB, 27 Mar 2026. 2015-16 is the latest census with published all-India results.

Once in 3 years

Choosing a crop the soil suits

A Soil Health Card is a printed lab report, issued once every three years per holding. Over 25 crore have been distributed. Between cards, the choice of crop rests on habit and memory.

PIB factsheet, Aug 2025

Fertiliser by guesswork

India's nitrogen : phosphorus : potassium use in 2012-13, against the officially recommended 4 : 2 : 1 — heavily skewed to nitrogen (urea). The same paper argues the norm itself needs updating, which is why CropWise works from the crop's own dose, not the ratio.

Chand & Pavithra, EPW 2015, hosted by NITI Aayog

Advice in the wrong language

Hindi is the mother tongue of 43.6% of Indians — the largest single language, and still less than half. Advice written only in Hindi or English misses most farmers.

Census of India 2011, Paper 1 of 2018 (Language), Statement 4 — copy hosted by Down To Earth (the census site's original link has moved).

How CropWise answers each

  • Crop: soil estimated for any town from ISRIC SoilGrids plus live weather → three crops, each with the reasons behind it. Get a crop plan
  • Fertiliser: the crop's recommended dose turned into bags of urea, DAP and MOP for your acres, with nearby shops from OpenStreetMap. See a sample plan
  • Mandi: markets ranked by what you keep after transport, not by headline price. Try the calculator
  • Language: KrishiMitra answers in six languages, by voice or text, with the source of every answer shown. Ask KrishiMitra
  • Trust: accuracy, retrieval and guardrail results are measured and published. How well it works

What it does not do yet

  • It is a working prototype. It has not been field-tested with farmers, and it has no user numbers to report.
  • The crop model is trained on a public Kaggle teaching dataset, not on Indian field trials.
  • Soil comes from SoilGrids, a ~250 m satellite-and-model estimate. A Soil Health Card is more accurate, and CropWise uses one when you enter it.
  • Mandi prices are placeholders, clearly labelled, until a data.gov.in key is configured.
  • For anything that matters — pests, disease, a costly input — confirm with your Krishi Vigyan Kendra or the Kisan Call Centre, 1800-180-1551.
