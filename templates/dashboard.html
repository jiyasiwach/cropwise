What this app will not do

It will not invent a number to fill a gap. Three examples you can check:

  • Phosphorus and potassium are left blank unless you upload a soil test — no map publishes them, and the earlier version of this app guessed them.
  • Soil under a town is reported as sampled from nearby farmland, with the distance, because satellites do not map built-up land.
  • Mandi prices are labelled MOCK until a data.gov.in key is set.
